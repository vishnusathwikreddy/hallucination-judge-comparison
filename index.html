How to read the tables

Base / RL
The instruction-tuned model without training / after 300 GRPO steps with LoRA, where the only reward is read from probes on the frozen model’s hidden states (section 8). Greedy captions, step 300 unless noted.
Matched length
Each base caption is cut to its paired RL caption’s token count. Shorter captions contain fewer errors, so a model cannot win here by saying less.
Judged errors
Errors per caption found by the gpt-5.1 judge (wrong objects, attributes, relations, counts). The standard prompt does not flag a hedged claim unless the thing is clearly absent; the hedge-strict prompt judges a hedged claim as if it were stated plainly. Clean captions: share with no judged error.
CHAIR, recall
Reference CHAIR (Rohrbach et al., OPERA’s chair.py) on COCO segmentation plus the objects named in the five human captions. CHAIRS: captions naming an absent object; CHAIRI: wrong object mentions; recall: the image’s objects the caption names. Wrong objects and precision: our synonym matcher on the segmentation labels.
Δ and CI
RL minus base, paired over images, 95% bootstrap interval underneath (clean captions: McNemar p). Mean rows give the standard deviation across seeds. Green: better and the interval excludes zero (on every seed, for a mean). Red: worse and excludes zero.
Reward check
Before RL, on probe-test captions. Rank: of two same-image captions cut to K five-token groups, how often the reward prefers the one with fewer wrong COCO objects (0.5 = chance). Coverage: among such pairs with equally many wrong objects, how often it prefers the one naming more annotated objects (below 0.5, naming less pays). Judge: agreement with the judge’s error counts. Filler: reward change, in within-image standard deviations, when every group is replaced with filler; drop flagged / unflagged: when the judge-flagged or the unflagged groups are. Passes: rank above 0.6 at the main K, filler at most 0, dropping flagged groups pays, dropping unflagged costs.

8. Method and reward parameters

Reward
R = Σ max(0, c − τ)·(θ − po) − λ·Σ max(0, ph − τr) over five-token groups, read from layer-⌊3L/4⌋ hidden states of the frozen base model. c: claim probe (does this group newly name an object?); po: object probe (is the named object absent?), trained on COCO annotations; ph: judge-trained hallucination probe.
Length-matched GRPO
Each sampled caption is scored against the other captions of the same image cut to its own length, so stopping early earns nothing by itself.
Training
300 steps, LoRA, lr 2×10−5, 8 samples per image, 32 per step, β = 0.04.
Offline check (Qwen2.5-VL-3B, pmG)
Rank 0.66 at K = 30, coverage 0.63, judge agreement 0.59 (judge vs itself: 0.70).
