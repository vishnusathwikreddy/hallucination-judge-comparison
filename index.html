First GRPO results on the new data: Qwen2.5-VL-3B

Update, 23 Oct 2026. The published probe reward (θ = 0.5, claim floor 0.35), retrained on the new gpt-5.1 labels, then 300 GRPO steps with LoRA, two seeds. Evaluated on 1,000 held-out COCO val2014 images, greedy captions, judged by gpt-5.1. Matched length cuts each base caption to its paired RL caption’s token count, so a model cannot win just by saying less. Step 300 was fixed as the primary checkpoint before training; step 250 (the old run’s operating point) is shown as secondary.

Takeaway

Two effects, consistent across both seeds and both checkpoints. Recall at matched length goes up (+0.9 to +2.6pp): at the same number of tokens, the RL model names more of the objects that are actually in the image, and names them earlier. That is a real change in content, and it reproduces the old run’s +1.6pp. Fewer judged hallucinations appear only against the full-length base. At matched length the findings are unchanged (−0.06 to −0.00), and object precision does not move: the extra objects come with errors in the same proportion. The old run’s −0.375 at matched length does not replicate in these two seeds.

Next: caption the new test images with the old run’s step-250 adapter. If it still beats the base at matched length here, the difference is in how this run was trained; if not, the old result was partly specific to its test set or seed.

Which model should label hallucinations?

The probe that becomes our RL reward is trained on span-level hallucination labels produced by an LLM judge. Before labelling 68,400 captions, we compared three candidate judges on the same 30 captions: gpt-5.1 (the judge our earlier results were built on), gpt-5.4-mini and gpt-5.4-nano.

Takeaway

gpt-5.4-nano flags ~2.7× as many statements as gpt-5.1 and never returns a clean caption; gpt-5.4-mini flags ~1.7× as many objects. On the captions where the judges disagree most, the extra flags from both smaller models are mostly statements that are plainly visible in the image (see the spot-check below). gpt-5.1 is the only judge close to the precision the pipeline was designed around, so it is the candidate for labelling, at a higher cost.

Spot-check: the three captions with the largest disagreement

  • Pizza table (Gemma 4 12B caption). gpt-5.4-mini and nano flag the second pizza with red sauce on the left edge, the salads and the slice on the bottom plate, all of which are visible. A few of their flags are defensible: the yellow topping looks more like squash than “yellow peppers”, the bottom-left plate shows a fork but no knife, and the “empty” top-right plate holds utensils and crumbs. gpt-5.1 flags one item, the top-left salad, which appears to be present.
  • City street (Qwen2.5-VL-3B caption). gpt-5.4-mini flags the bicycle (a cyclist is in the middle of the road), the leafless trees, the pedestrians and the lane markings, all visible; nano flags the black car’s lit brake lights, which are lit. Both small models flag “benches and trash cans”, which are not clearly visible, plausibly a real error that gpt-5.1 missed.
  • Children’s party (Qwen2.5-VL-7B caption). gpt-5.4-mini flags the pizza on the table, the adults, the mirror and the person taking a photo, and the wooden floor; nano flags “children actively eating” and the peace-sign decorations. All are visible. gpt-5.1 flags nothing.

These three were chosen because the judges disagree most, so they show the failure mode rather than estimate an error rate. Every caption, image and finding is below to check.

All 30 captions

Five greedy captions from each of six captioning models, sampled at random (seed 0) from held-out COCO images. Use the buttons on each card to highlight one judge’s flagged spans in the caption.

Method

Every judge received the identical request: the same system prompt (below), the caption in the same user template, the image as a base64 JPEG at detail: high, JSON mode, max_completion_tokens 4000, default temperature and reasoning effort. Only the model name differs (gpt-5.1, gpt-5.4-mini-2026-03-17, gpt-5.4-nano-2026-03-17). Captions are greedy, up to 512 tokens, from Qwen2.5-VL-3B/7B, LLaVA-1.5-7B/13B and Gemma 4 E4B/12B on COCO val2014 images held out from all training.
