Qwen2.5-VL: probe-reward GRPO

The numbers for the paper: on Qwen2.5-VL-3B, the final reward (pmG) over three seeds, its two ablations, what the removed errors are, and capability on three out-of-distribution benchmarks; then the same recipe on Qwen2.5-VL-7B (section 6, three seeds). Captioning on 1,000 held-out COCO images. Updated 26 Oct 2026.

Main result, 3 seeds

At matched length, −0.55 judged findings per caption (−27%) on average, significant on every seed; −0.57 with a hedge-strict judge. Clean captions 34% → 45%. No significant recall change at matched length on any seed.

Ablations

Without length matching the policy cuts captions to 120 tokens and loses the judge-free object gain. With the judge-trained probe pricing objects, the judged gain doubles but recall drops.

Caveat

Part of the drop is captions stating fewer colours and materials (−26%), which the judge does not penalise. Object errors fall 21% while 3% fewer objects are named.

How to read the tables

Base / pmG
Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct without training / after 300 GRPO steps with LoRA, where the only reward is a probe on the frozen model’s hidden states (method below). Greedy captions, evaluated at step 300, the checkpoint fixed before training.
Matched length
Each base caption is cut to its paired RL caption’s token count. Shorter captions contain fewer errors, so a model cannot win here just by saying less.
Judged findings
Errors per caption found by the gpt-5.1 judge: wrong objects, attributes, relations and counts. The standard prompt does not flag a hedged claim (“appears to be…”) unless the thing is clearly absent; the hedge-strict prompt judges a hedged claim exactly as if it were stated plainly.
CHAIR
Standard CHAIR (Rohrbach et al., the chair.py used by OPERA) on COCO segmentation and caption ground truth. CHAIRS: captions naming at least one object not in the image. CHAIRI: object mentions that are wrong. Recall: the image’s objects the caption names. Wrong objects and precision use our synonym matcher on the segmentation labels.
Δ and CI
pmG minus base, paired over images, 95% bootstrap interval (10,000 draws) underneath; the mean row gives the standard deviation across seeds. Green: better and the interval excludes zero (every seed, for the mean). Red: worse and excludes zero.

Reading it

Length matching is not what lowers the judged error count: without it the same reward still gives −0.54 at matched length. What it does is keep captions long (−25 instead of −83 tokens), so far less recall is lost at full length, and it is needed for the judge-free wrong-object gain. Pricing objects with the judge-trained probe instead of the object probe gives a larger judged gain, also under the hedge-strict judge, but costs recall even at matched length and removes the most attribute detail (section 4); its reward also pays for filler offline, so pmG is the main method.

pmG’s attribute errors fall about as fast as its colour and material words: most of that part is saying less, not saying it more accurately. 6. Qwen2.5-VL-7B: the same recipe on a larger model

Same reward parameters, GRPO settings and evaluation as for Qwen2.5-VL-3B; the probes are trained the same way on the 7B model’s own hidden states (layer 21). The 7B base already makes fewer errors: 1.52 judged findings per caption and 44.2% clean captions at full length, against 2.27 and 31.5% for 3B. Before training, pmG’s reward prefers the caption with fewer wrong COCO objects 66% of the time (3B: 66%), and the object probe’s AUROC is 0.92. No hedge-strict judging for 7B.

Reading it

All three seeds lower judged findings per caption at matched length (−0.28 (−20%), −0.09 (−7%), −0.18 (−13%)), significant on 2 of 3 (on 3B every seed gave −0.53 to −0.59), and all three raise the share of clean captions (McNemar p = 0.002, p = 0.005, p = 0.09). The judge-free object metrics (CHAIR, wrong objects) do not change significantly on any seed, and recall at matched length does not fall; it rises slightly. The transfer holds in direction but is smaller and less consistent than on 3B, where the base had more errors to remove.

7. Method

Reward
R = Σ max(0, c − 0.35)·(0.3 − h) − 0.1·Σ max(0, r − 0.35), over five-token groups of the caption, read from layer-27 hidden states of the frozen base model. c: claim probe (does this group newly name an object?); h: object probe; r: judge-trained hallucination probe.
Object probe
Trained on COCO ground truth for the groups that first name an object: is this object in the image? AUROC 0.93 on held-out images, against 0.66 for the judge-trained probe on the same mentions. COCO labels are used only offline to train it.
Length-matched GRPO
Each sampled caption is scored against the other captions of the same image cut to its own length, so stopping early earns nothing by itself.
Training
300 steps, LoRA, lr 2×10−5, 8 samples per image, 32 per step, 200-token budget, β = 0.04. One RTX 6000 Ada per seed, 2 h 16 min.
Offline check
Among same-length captions of an image, the reward prefers the one with fewer wrong COCO objects 66% of the time and agrees with an independent judge run 60% of the time (judge vs itself: 70%).
